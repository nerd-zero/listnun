# Lists and forms

## Lists

A list is a group of subscribers you send campaigns to. Open **Lists › All lists** to see them, with how many subscribers each has and how many are still waiting to confirm.

Each list has two settings that matter:

| Setting | Options |
| :------ | :------ |
| **Type** | **Public** lists can be added to signup forms and appear on subscribers' preferences page. **Private** lists are for your own segments; people can't join them themselves. |
| **Opt-in** | **Single**: people are subscribed straight away. **Double**: each new subscriber gets an email with a confirmation link, and campaigns only go to subscribers who confirmed. |

Use double opt-in for lists people sign up to on your website. It keeps typos and fake addresses off your list and helps your emails reach inboxes.

From a list's **⋯** menu you can view its subscribers, import into it, or send an opt-in campaign that asks unconfirmed subscribers to confirm.

Deleting a list doesn't delete its subscribers. They just stop being on that list.

## Signup forms

**Lists › Forms** builds a signup form you can paste into your website.

1. Tick the public lists people should join. Private lists can't be added; make a list public first if you need it.
2. Choose whether to **Ask for a name**.
3. Optionally **Add a captcha** to keep bots out. This needs a captcha set up under **Settings › Security**.
4. Optionally set a **Redirect URL** to send people to a thank-you page after they sign up. The URL must be allowed under **Settings › Security**.
5. Click **Copy HTML** and paste it into your site.

The preview shows the form as it will look. It's unstyled, so it picks up your website's own CSS.

There's also a ready-made **Public subscription page** on your workspace's address that lists all your public lists, if you'd rather link to that.

## What subscribers see

Every campaign includes an unsubscribe link. Depending on your **Settings › Privacy**, subscribers can also:

- manage which of your public lists they're on,
- download the data you hold about them,
- delete their data.

People who unsubscribe land on a page where they can resubscribe if it was a mistake.
