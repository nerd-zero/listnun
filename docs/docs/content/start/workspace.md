# A tour of your workspace

## Signing in

Your workspace has its own address, such as `https://yourcompany.listnun.app/admin`. Sign in with your username and password, or with single sign-on if your workspace uses it. You can pick the language at the bottom of the sign-in page.

If your workspace requires two-step verification and you haven't set it up yet, you'll be taken to your profile to set it up before anything else. See [Your profile and two-step verification](../guides/team.md#your-profile-and-two-step-verification).

## The sidebar

| Section | What's in it |
| :------ | :----------- |
| **Dashboard** | Subscriber growth, recent campaigns and activity for the period you choose. |
| **Lists** | **All lists** and **Forms**, where you build a signup form for your website. |
| **Subscribers** | **All subscribers**, **Import** and **Bounces**. |
| **Campaigns** | **All campaigns**, **Create new**, **Media**, **Templates** and **Analytics**. |
| **Developers** | API keys, email templates, send test, message log, email branding and webhooks. See [Developers](../developers/index.md). |
| **Workspace** | **Users** (your team and their roles) and **Settings**. |

Click a section with an arrow to open or close it. listnun remembers which sections you left open.

At the bottom, **Collapse** shrinks the sidebar to a narrow strip of icons to give your work more room; **Expand** brings it back. That's remembered too. **Billing and workspace** opens the listnun console, where you manage your plan.

## The top bar

- The breadcrumb shows where you are.
- **Refresh** reloads the data on the page without reloading the whole app.
- The moon and sun icon switches between dark and light mode.
- Your initials open the account menu: your profile and signing out.

## Phones and tablets

On a narrow screen the sidebar becomes a menu behind the menu button at the top left.
